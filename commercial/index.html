Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Erie Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched fast for emergency repairs throughout Erie and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done overnight or before opening to limit downtime.
